About the game

A huge storm blew away the whole town, residents and all. It’s up to you to rebuild Pláňov – house by house, street by street.

Build your village

Cottages, a bakery, an inn, a church, a railway station and a manor. Over 60 buildings and decorations, roads, a river with bridges and land you can keep buying.

Bring the residents back

New buildings bring the residents back. Each one has their own personality and job – send them on tasks and collect the rewards.

Friends and visits

Add friends by their nickname, peek into their villages and see whose Pláňov looks best.

Levels and badges

Building and working earns you experience. Every level unlocks something new – all the way to level 200. And you collect badges for your achievements.

How to start

Nothing to download and nothing to pay. All you need is a browser on your computer or phone.

Click PLAY

The game loads right in your browser – Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.

Create an account

Choose a nickname and password. Your village is saved, so you can switch between your phone and computer.

Add it to your home screen

On iPhone in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android in Chrome: ⋮ → Add to Home screen. Pláňov then opens like an app.

Or the Android app

Want the Android app? Just ask the author of the game.

A free online game with no download

A building game in your browser

Pláňov is a free online game you start right in your browser – no download, no install. City building here starts with a small cottage and ends with a village full of houses, shops, a church, a railway station and a manor house. Build your village online in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.

A relaxing game for phone and PC

You don’t have to play for hours. Send residents on a job, collect money from your houses and come back later – your village keeps growing. Progress is saved in the cloud, so you can switch between phone and computer.

A game with Czech charm

The Mole Inn, a bakery, a fire station, football with the Czech league and Christmas with an advent calendar. Pláňov is a browser game by a Czech author – playable in English, German, Czech and Slovak.
