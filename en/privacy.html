Privacy policy

Pláňov is a free game made for fun and run by its author, Aleš Jaroš.

What we store:

  • e-mail – only for logging in and recovering a forgotten password. Other players can't see it.
  • password – stored only in encrypted form; nobody knows it.
  • nickname – visible to other players (for friends).
  • your saved game – your town, money and level.
  • your friends list and friend requests.

What it's for: only so you can play on different devices and have friends. We don't sell or share your data with anyone, and we don't use ads or tracking.

Where it's stored: on Supabase servers in Ireland (European Union).

Deletion: you can delete your account and all its data yourself in Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Questions: contact the author of the game.

Visit statistics: planov.cz counts visits anonymously using GoatCounter – no cookies and no personal data. We only see totals, such as how many people came and from which website.
